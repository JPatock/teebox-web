Par Pal Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and chosen username. When you use Par Pal we store the content you post: round scores, scorecards, photos, videos, comments, likes, follows, and an optional display name, bio, and profile photo.

How we use it

Your content exists to be shared with other Par Pal users — that's the point of the app. Your posts, profile, and stats are visible to signed-in users. We use your email only for account access (sign-in and account recovery). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (our database and storage provider) on servers in the United States, protected by row-level security so users can only modify their own content.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated content at any time from the Profile tab ("Delete account"). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
